Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MyViewLab Scanner Drive accesses and uses Google Drive data.

1. Information Accessed

The application requests Google's drive.file OAuth scope to access files created by or explicitly authorized for use by the application.

2. How Information Is Used

Google Drive access is used to upload and update CSV reports generated by the stock market scanner.

3. Data Storage and Security

OAuth authorization tokens are stored locally on the authorized computer and are not published on this website.

Google Drive files remain within the user's Google Drive account and are subject to its configured access and sharing permissions.

4. Data Sharing

The application does not sell Google user data or use Google Drive data for advertising.

Files stored in shared Google Drive folders may be accessible to other users according to the folder's sharing settings.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Users can delete scanner-generated CSV files from Google Drive and remove locally stored application authorization tokens.
