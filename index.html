MyViewLab Scanner Drive

MyViewLab Scanner Drive is a personal stock market research utility that analyzes US stock market data and generates CSV reports containing technical screening results.

Google Drive Integration

The application uses Google Drive OAuth authorization to upload and update scanner-generated CSV reports in a user-selected Google Drive folder.

It requests the drive.file OAuth scope to limit access to files created by or explicitly authorized for the application.

Application Purpose

The application is intended for personal stock market research and automated report management.

It does not place trades through Google Drive.
